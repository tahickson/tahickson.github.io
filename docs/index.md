<h2>NEWS</h2>
<p>
<i><b>First MBSP Open Working Group Meeting. </b></i> In early January of 2026 we held or first meeting of the working group that has agreed to oversee and/or participate in the development of the database. In this meeting we agreed to use Basecamp as the project management software for development and that we will hold regular meetings of an open working group to get feedback on the project. We have begun to develop a set of guiding principals for the project that govern how data are ingested, reviewed, and managed. We are also working on a deployment schedule. Contact us if you are interested in participating!
